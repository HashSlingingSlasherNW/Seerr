📊 Theme Selection Guide

Emerald-Gold: Warm, energetic, inviting. Best for community-focused applications, media consumption platforms, and user-friendly services. The emerald-to-gold transition evokes growth and excitement.

Indigo-Rose: Cool, sophisticated, mystical. Best for premium applications, creative platforms, and modern tech services. Suggests elegance and contemporary design.

Animation Styles:
• Refined Base: Professional, minimal motion
• Bouncy Ripple: Playful, interactive, high visual feedback
• Wave Flow: Organic, sophisticated, fluid motion
• Particles: Ambient, ethereal, background motion
• Glitch: Modern, digital, technical aesthetic
• Rotating: Smooth, polished, premium feel
